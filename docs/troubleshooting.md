# Troubleshooting

## A source does not appear

- Confirm that the source is installed or added using the correct method for its ecosystem.
- Check whether the source format is supported by your Nekomo version.
- Restart the app and check for available updates.

## A source opens but content does not load

- Check whether the source itself is still maintained and available.
- Test another item or catalogue entry to determine whether the issue is source-specific.
- Check your network connection and any source-specific requirements.

## Titles or artwork are missing

Metadata may come from a separate catalogue provider rather than the streaming or reading source. Check the selected metadata provider and whether the item can be found there.

## When reporting a problem

Include Nekomo version, Android version/device, source ecosystem, source name and version, steps to reproduce, and relevant logs. Remove credentials, cookies, tokens, and personal information.
